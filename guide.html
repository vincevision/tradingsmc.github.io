How to study

  1. Follow the numbers.Do not jump ahead. Later pages use words and rules from earlier ones.
  2. One step per sitting.Read the page once fully. Enlarge every chart and find each mark the text mentions.
  3. Answer the check question.Each step has one. If you cannot answer it without looking, read the page again.
  4. Tick “I have studied this page”.The box is at the bottom of every page. Your progress is saved in this browser and shown here.
  5. Press “Next”.The bar at the bottom of each page links to the next step and the one before.
  6. Ask when stuck.The “Ask” button at the bottom right answers questions about any page.

If you open a page out of order

The bar at the bottom tells you which earlier step is still open and links back to it. Nothing is locked, because you will want to look things up later. The order matters the first time through.

After the path

What to do when all 33 are ticked

  1. Backtest.In replay mode, take 50 setups with the live checklist. Record every one, including the losses and the ones you skipped.
  2. Review.After every 20 trades, count wins, losses and average R. Look for the step you break most often.
  3. Demo.Trade the same rules on a demo account in real time for at least a month.
  4. Small live.Only then use real money, at 0.5% risk per trade or less.
  5. Daily.Read the short version of the notes before each session and use the checklist on every trade.

Finishing the path is not the same as being profitable

The pages teach a routine and show it in chosen winning examples. Whether it makes money for you is something only your own recorded trades can show.
