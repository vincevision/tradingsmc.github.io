Concept

Top-down analysis: 4H, 1H, 30m, 5m

One long, read from the largest chart to the smallest. Each timeframe answers one question and hands the next one a smaller area to look at. The 4H gives the trend. The 1H says the pullback may be ending. The 30m gives the zone. The 5m gives the entry. By the time an order is placed, four charts agree.

How to read this page

X marks a liquidity sweep. SSL and BSL are sell-side and buy-side liquidity. EQH is equal highs. $$ marks resting liquidity. The slide captions are quoted under each chart. The slides name no pair and show no price or time, so anything beyond the captions is my reading. Reward-to-risk is measured from the position box.

4H · Direction

4H: bullish, and pulling back

What the slide shows

  1. Three breaks of structure upward. Higher highs and higher lows.
  2. A peak, then a slow decline. It ends with one sharp drop.
  3. Price now. Back near the level of the last BoS, well above the lows of the trend.

The question this chart answers

Which side? Long. The trend is up and no 4H low has been broken. The decline is a pullback, which means price is getting cheaper inside an uptrend.

The 4H does not say where the pullback ends. It could stop here or go much deeper.

Rule to carry forward

Start on the 4H. Write down one word, long or short, from the last breaks of structure. If you cannot, there is no trade today on this pair.

1H · Timing

1H: the pullback shifts back up

What the slide shows

  1. The pullback in detail. Lower highs and lower lows on the 1H.
  2. BoS down. Price breaks below the low at the left of the chart. The stops under that low are taken.
  3. A new low (SSL). The lowest point of the pullback.
  4. MSS. One large bullish candle closes above the last lower high.
  5. BSL. A line at the high the 1H decline began from.

The question this chart answers

Is the pullback finished? Possibly. The slide says “might”. The 1H was making lower highs. It has now closed above one, straight after taking an old low. That is a sweep followed by a shift, on the timeframe one below the trend.

Two levels are now fixed. The SSL low is where the idea is wrong. The BSL high is where price is likely headed if it is right.

Rule to carry forward

On the 1H, wait for the pullback to take a low and then close above its last lower high. Before that, the 4H trend is not a reason to buy.

30m · Location

30m: the order block the shift came from

What is marked

  • MSS. The same shift as on the 1H, seen closer.
  • 30m OB. A box at the base of the candle that made it.
  • SSL. The pullback low, under the order block.
  • $$. A short line just above the order block, at the low price is making on its way back down.

The question this chart answers

Where do I look for the entry? At the 30m order block. It is the origin of the move that shifted structure, and the low beneath it has already swept an older low.

The $$ line is a warning. A small low is forming just above the block. Buyers who enter there will have stops under it, between price and the zone. Expect that low to be taken.

The caption gives a choice here. See 30m or 5m entry? below.

5m · Confirmation

5m: sweep and shift at the 30m order block

What the slide shows

  1. The drift back down. Lower highs line up under a dotted line with $$$. That is trendline liquidity above price.
  2. The sweep (X). One candle takes the internal low and reaches the top of the 30m OB.
  3. MSS. The next push closes above the last 5m high.
  4. IMB and 5m OB. The push leaves an imbalance. A narrow order block is drawn at its base, just above the 30m OB.
  5. EQH. After the shift, price makes two highs at the same level and turns down.

The question this chart answers

Is the zone being defended right now? Yes. The caption lists four things in order: internal low swept, imbalance filled, 30m OB tapped, MSS. That is the full sequence again, on the smallest chart, inside the zone the larger charts pointed to.

The equal highs are liquidity. They are close, clean and untouched, so they become the target.

5m · The order

Entry, stop and target from the caption

What is marked

  • Entry: the top edge of the 5m OB.
  • Stop: under the low of the sweep, labelled SSL on this slide. It sits at the top of the 30m OB.
  • Target: the equal highs.

Reading the box

The target is about 2.5 times the stop. It is the nearest liquidity above, not the 1H BSL, which is much further away. The post takes the close, likely target and leaves the far one.

The stop is under the 5m sweep low. It is not under the 30m OB. If price goes deeper into the 30m block, this trade is stopped even though the 30m idea is still alive.

Result

Result: an edge fill, one deep wick, then the equal highs

What happened

  1. A slow drift down. Price slid from the equal highs back toward the 5m OB.
  2. The fill. Several candles sat on the top edge of the block.
  3. One deep wick. A single wick went through nearly the whole 5m OB. It stopped above the stop.
  4. The rally. A few large candles carried price to the equal highs, and a wick went through them.

What to take from it

  • The block was narrow and the wick went through all of it. A stop under the block, not under the sweep low, would have been hit.
  • The target was liquidity that formed after the shift. Price took it and the chart ends there.

THE LOGIC IN ONE SENTENCE

Take direction from the 4H, timing from the 1H, the zone from the 30m and the entry from the 5m, and only when all four agree.

The choice in the caption

Enter at the 30m order block, or wait for the 5m?

The post says: “We can actually take a trade from this 30m OB, but for higher probability, we drop down to a lower timeframe for confirmation.” Both are valid. They are the two entry models applied one level down.

EntryTop edge of the 30m OB, placed in advanceTop edge of the 5m OB, placed after the 5m MSS
StopUnder the 30m OB, or under the SSL lowUnder the 5m sweep low

“High probability” is a claim, not a measurement

More agreement between timeframes should filter out weaker trades. It also means fewer trades and tighter stops, and tighter stops are hit by noise more often. The post gives no numbers. One example cannot show whether the 5m confirmation raises the win rate enough to pay for the trades it misses. Backtest both versions on the same setups and compare.

Order placement

Entry, stop loss and take profit

For the 5m version, as drawn on slide 5.

Entry

Buy limit at the top edge of the 5m OB.

  • The block is the base of the 5m MSS move, under its imbalance, just above the 30m OB.
  • Place the order when the 5m MSS candle closes.
  • On a 5m entry the spread is a real share of the stop. Allow for it.

Stop loss

Below the 5m sweep low, plus a buffer.

  • The caption calls it “below the internal low”. On slide 5 the box ends just under the SSL line.
  • Not just under the 5m OB. The result shows a wick through the whole block.
  • The wider choice is under the 1H SSL low. Use it with the 30m entry, not with this one.

Take profit

The equal highs (EQH). About 2.5R.

  • The nearest clean liquidity above the entry.
  • Set the order a little before the level.
  • The 1H BSL is the further target. The post does not use it. If you want to hold a part for it, close most at the EQH first.

Repeatable strategy

Step-by-step for the next trade

Written for longs. For shorts, mirror it. A “no” at any step ends the process.

  1. 4H: is structure breaking upward, and is price pulling back?Write “long”. If the 4H is unclear, stop.
  2. 1H: has the pullback taken a low and then closed above its last lower high?Mark the pullback low (SSL) and the high above (BSL).
  3. 30m: draw the order block at the base of that shift.Set an alert at its top edge. Mark any small low forming above it as liquidity.
  4. Decide now: 30m entry or 5m confirmation.Do not decide when price arrives.
  5. 5m: when price reaches the block, is an internal low swept?The sweep should reach the 30m OB.
  6. 5m: does a candle close above the last 5m high with an imbalance?Draw the 5m OB at the base of that move.
  7. Buy limit at the 5m OB, stop under the sweep low.Target the nearest clean highs above.
  8. Does it pay at least 1:2?If not, skip it.
  9. Size, place, hold, record.Risk 0.5–1%. Screenshot all four timeframes.
