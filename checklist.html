Check the trade, one step at a time

Use this while you analyse. Choose the model and direction, then work down the nine steps. Each step unlocks when the one before it is complete, so nothing gets skipped. On the chart steps you can add a screenshot and have the AI check it against the step.

  • 9 steps
  • Zone model or sweep model
  • Optional AI screenshot check

How it works

Tick each box only when it is true on your chart. To use the AI check, add a screenshot that shows the timeframe, your lines and the price axis, then press “Check with AI”. It answers “looks right”, “cannot tell” or “something does not match”, with what it saw. Your progress is saved in this browser. Screenshots are sent to Groq for the check and are not stored on this site.

What the AI check is and is not

It is a second pair of eyes that reads a picture. It can misread a chart, miss a wick or agree with a mistake. Treat “looks right” as “nothing obviously wrong”, not as approval to trade. If it disagrees with you, look again; you can still continue on your own judgement.

The steps are explained in full on the zone model and sweep model pages. The rules behind them are in the playbook.

Educational material only, not financial advice. The AI check is a second opinion on a screenshot and can be wrong. It does not see live prices and does not tell you to buy or sell. The decision and the risk are yours.
