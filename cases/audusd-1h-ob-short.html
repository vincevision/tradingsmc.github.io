Short From the 1H Order Block

A short on AUD/USD. The 1H runs above an old high, fails and shifts structure down, leaving an order block. Price climbs back into that block. On the 15m it sweeps a high there, shifts down and breaks structure. The entry is the 15m order block at the origin of that drop, filled after the inducement high is taken. The target is the low below.

How to read this page

X marks a liquidity sweep. IDM is inducement. SSL is sell-side liquidity. IMB is an imbalance and OB an order block. The post has no caption and the slides carry only timeframe titles, so the reasoning is my reading of the charts. There is no price axis and the time axis cannot be read, so I give no pip distances or times. Reward-to-risk is measured from the position box.

Step 1 · Higher-timeframe bias and zone

1H: an old high is swept, structure shifts down

What happened

  1. An old high at the left. Price fell a long way from it, then spent the rest of the chart climbing back.
  2. The sweep (X). The rally pushed above that old high. It stayed above for a short while and made no further progress.
  3. MSS down. Price rolled over and closed below the last higher low of the rally.
  4. The 1H order block. A box at the last candles before the drop that made the shift.
  5. The return. Price bounces and is heading back up toward the block as the chart ends.

Why this matters

The rally looked like a recovery. It ended by taking the stops above the old high and then breaking its own structure. That is a sweep followed by a shift on the 1H, so the bias is short. The order block is where to look for the entry.

Rule to carry forward

When the 1H takes an old high and then closes below its last higher low, mark the order block the drop came from. Wait for price to return to it and change to the 15m.

Step 2 · Confirmation and plan

15m: sweep and shift inside the 1H order block

What happened

  1. The rally into the 1H OB. From the low at the far left, price climbs into the block.
  2. A sweep inside it (X). A 15m high is taken and price tops just above. The line across the chart marks that top.
  3. MSS. Price drifts down and closes below a 15m low.
  4. A spike to a lower high. Price jumps back into the lower part of the 1H OB. It stops short of the top.
  5. The drop. A sharp fall from that lower high leaves an imbalance (IMB). The 15 OB is drawn at its origin.
  6. BoS. After a pause, price breaks the low under the MSS and keeps falling.
  7. IDM. The pause left a small high between price and the 15m OB.

The position box

  • Entry: the bottom edge of the 15m OB.
  • Stop: above the lower high the drop came from. That is above the 15m OB and below the top of the rally.
  • Target: the SSL line, drawn from the low at the far left where the rally began. About 2.5 times the stop.

Price had already fallen a long way

When this plan was drawn, price had dropped most of the distance to the target and bounced. The order was still left in place. The target had not been touched and the IDM high and imbalance were still open above. Many traders would have cancelled here. See the note under “When to skip”.

Step 3 · Result

Result: inducement taken, edge fill, straight to the low

What happened

  1. The pullback. Price climbed back from the lows in a few large candles.
  2. IDM taken. It went through the small high from the pause. Anyone who sold there with a stop above it was taken out.
  3. IMB filled, OB touched. One wick reached just into the bottom edge of the 15m OB. The sell limit filled.
  4. The drop. Price fell almost without a pause to the SSL line and well through it.

What to take from it

  • The fill was a wick at the edge. An order placed deeper in the block would not have filled.
  • The stop was never approached.
  • The inducement high was the bait. The fill came one step above it.

THE LOGIC IN ONE SENTENCE

After a 1H sweep and shift down, let price return to the 1H order block, wait for the 15m to sweep and shift there, and sell the 15m order block toward the low.

This is the full sequence mirrored for a short and run on two timeframes. It is also the short-side twin of the second USD/JPY case.

Order placement

Entry, stop loss and take profit

Entry

Sell limit at the bottom edge of the 15m OB.

  • The block is at the origin of the sharp drop, above the imbalance.
  • Only a wick reached it. The edge is the price that filled.
  • Do not sell the IDM high below it. That high was taken first.
  • Sells fill on the bid, so no spread needs adding to the limit price.

Stop loss

Above the swing high over the 15m OB, plus spread and a buffer.

  • The grey box ends just above the lower high the drop came from.
  • If price goes back above that high, the 15m shift has failed.
  • Buy stops trigger on the ask. Add the spread to the stop price.
  • The wider alternative is above the top of the rally, the line across the chart. It lowers the ratio.

Take profit

The SSL line: the low the rally started from. About 2.5R.

  • It is the low left by the 1H shift, where the climb back to the order block began.
  • Price went well through it. The plan still exits at the line.
  • Set the order a few pips above the level.

Managing the position

The post shows a single entry and a single exit. The points below are my suggested handling, not something marked on the slides.

  • While waiting: cancel if price touches the SSL line before filling you, or if a 15m candle closes above the 15m OB.
  • Once filled: leave the stop alone until price breaks the low made before the pullback.
  • News: check the calendar for AUD and USD releases before leaving an order or a position open.

Position size

Fix the money at risk first, then let the stop distance decide the lot size.

Lots = money at risk ÷ (stop in pips × pip value per lot)

On AUD/USD one standard lot moves $10 per pip.

Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk = $100. Stop of 12 pips gives 100 ÷ (12 × 10) ≈ 0.83 lots. At 1:2.5 the target pays about $250. The 12 pips is an illustration. The slides show no prices.

Filters

When this setup should be skipped

Do not take it if

  • The 1H has not shifted down, or has closed back above the swept high.
  • Price has not returned to the 1H OB.
  • A 1H candle closes above the 1H OB.
  • The 15m breaks down without first sweeping a high.
  • The drop is slow, with no imbalance.
  • The 15m OB has already been touched since the drop.
  • Price has already reached the target before the order fills.
  • The low is closer than 1:2.
  • High-impact AUD or USD news is due while the order rests.

This trade had all of these

  • A 1H sweep of an old high and a 1H MSS down.
  • A return into the 1H order block.
  • A 15m sweep, MSS and BoS inside it, with an imbalance.
  • A 15m order block at the origin of the drop.
  • An inducement high taken before the fill.
  • An untouched low about 2.5R away.

Keep this in proportion

Before the fill, price had already covered most of the distance to the target and come all the way back. Here that return was the pullback that filled the order. On another day the same picture is a move that has spent its energy, and the return is the start of a reversal through the block. The slides give no way to tell the two apart in advance. If you take trades like this, log them separately and see how they do.
