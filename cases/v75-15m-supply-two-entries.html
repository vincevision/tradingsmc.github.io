How to read this page

These are your screenshots in TradingView replay mode, in the order you sent them, with your own marks: bos, MSS, X for a level that was swept, $$$ for liquidity, Protected swing, grey boxes for supply and a teal box for demand. You sent no notes, so the reasoning is my reading. Order prices are those printed on the price axis; other prices are approximate. The position size is not shown.

A synthetic index is not a market

Volatility 75 (1s) prices are generated by the provider, not by buyers and sellers, so sweeps and zones here cannot have the causes this site describes, and random prices draw them too. The first Volatility 75 case explains this in full. Treat this page as practice in the routine, and keep its result out of your statistics for real markets.

Step 1 · Structure

A low breaks: mark the protected swing and the zone

What the chart shows

  1. X marks. Earlier highs that were taken, each followed by a lower low.
  2. bos, MSS, bos. The 15m is making lower lows. The last bos, near 4,034, has just been closed below.
  3. Protected swing, about 4,087. The high that the breaking move came from.
  4. The grey box, about 4,062 to 4,085. The last up candles before that fall: the supply zone.
  5. The teal zone at the bottom. A higher-timeframe demand area, below about 3,965.

Why it matters

The moment the low breaks, three things are fixed: the direction (short), the level that proves the idea wrong (the protected swing) and the place to sell from (the supply that caused the break). Nothing is traded yet. Price is at 4,028, well below the zone, and selling here would mean a stop of almost 60 points.

Rule to carry forward

When a low breaks, mark the protected swing and the supply zone straight away. Then wait for price to come back to the zone.

Step 2 · Target and return

Mark the target, then let price return

What the chart shows

  1. The fall. From the zone price drops to about 3,966.
  2. $$$ at about 3,965. The line under that low. Stops rest below it, and it sits on the top edge of the teal demand zone.
  3. The rally. Price climbs back about 110 points into the supply zone and reaches about 4,078.
  4. The first reaction. It drops to about 4,025, then comes back to the bottom edge of the zone at 4,064.

Why it matters

The target is chosen before the entry: the low, which is both liquidity and the edge of the opposing zone. The rally is large, but the protected swing at 4,087 has not been touched, so the short idea is intact.

Note that the zone has now been entered once. The entry in the next step is on the second approach. A zone is strongest on its first touch.

Rule to carry forward

A deep pullback does not cancel the idea. A close beyond the protected swing does.

Step 3 · First order

Sell at the bottom edge of the zone

What the chart shows

  1. Entry 4,062.63. The bottom edge of the supply.
  2. Stop 4,088.52. About a point and a half above the protected swing.
  3. Target 3,965.15. On the $$$ line, at the top of the teal zone.
  4. The numbers. About 26 points of risk for about 97 of reward, roughly 1:3.8.

Why it matters

All three orders come from marks made earlier. The stop is beyond the protected swing, so it is only hit if the idea is wrong. The target is the first opposing level.

This entry has no shift behind it. Price reacted from the zone once but did not break a 15m low before this sell, so it is an entry on the touch.

Rule to carry forward

Entry at the near edge of the zone, stop beyond the protected swing, target at the first opposing liquidity. If the three do not give 1:2, skip it.

Step 4 · Second setup

A sweep and a shift give a second entry

What the chart shows

  1. X near 4,078. After the first entry, price pushes up again and takes the high of the first reaction. The first trade is about 15 points against at this moment.
  2. MSS near 4,023. Price then falls and closes below the low of the reaction. The 15m has shifted down from inside the zone.
  3. The fall stops at about 3,978, some 13 points short of the target, and price bounces.
  4. A new grey box, about 4,058 to 4,086. The supply left by the move that made the shift.
  5. Second sell. Entry 4,058.67, stop 4,086.94, target 3,967.27. About 28 points of risk for 91 of reward, roughly 1:3.2.

Why it matters

This is the full sequence from the notes, mirrored for a short: a sweep of a high inside the zone, a shift down, then a sell at the supply that caused the shift. The first entry was taken on the touch. This one has confirmation.

The first trade came within 13 points of its target and then gave most of it back. Nothing in the plan said to close early, so it stays open.

Rule to carry forward

The strongest entry is the one after a sweep and a shift inside the zone. If you take only one of the two entries, take this one.

Two positions, one idea

If the first short is still open when the second fills, both stops are near 4,087 and both can be hit together. For risk, that is one trade of double size. The scaling rules say to size the two so that together they stay within your limit for one trade, for example half the normal risk on each.

Step 5 · Result

The second sell fills and both targets are reached

What the chart shows

  1. The rally. Price climbs from about 3,978 back to about 4,060 and touches the second entry at 4,058.67.
  2. The first trade is back within 3 points of its own entry at this moment, after being about 85 points in profit.
  3. The fall. Price drops through 4,000 and through the $$$ line. Both targets, 3,967.27 and 3,965.15, are reached.
  4. After the target. Price continues into the teal zone to about 3,930.

Why it matters

The second entry filled by a point or two. An order placed deeper in the zone would have been missed.

Price went another 35 points past the targets. That is not a mistake in the plan: the target was the top of a demand zone, and the plan was to be out before it.

Rule to carry forward

Take the profit at the level you marked. What price does after the target is not part of the trade.

Order placement

Entry, stop loss and take profit

Entry

Sell limits at the bottom edge of the supply: 4,062.63, then 4,058.67.

  • Both are at the first price where the zone can react.
  • The first is on the return to the zone. The second follows a sweep and a shift.
  • The second filled by a point or two.

Stop loss

4,088.52 for the first, 4,086.94 for the second. About 26 and 28 points.

  • The first stop is above the protected swing at about 4,087. That is the right side of it.
  • The second is a fraction below that line. Put it above the swing as well, with a few points of buffer and the spread.
  • The highest price after the first entry was about 4,078, some 10 points under the stops.

Take profit

3,965.15 and 3,967.27, at the low above the demand zone. About 97 and 91 points.

  • The low is liquidity and the edge of the first opposing zone.
  • The second target, two points higher, is the better placement: a short closes at the buy price, which is above the chart price.
  • Price went about 35 points further. The plan was to be out before the demand.

Position size

Size = money at risk ÷ (stop in points × value of one point per lot), using the point value and smallest lot from your platform’s contract specification. With two entries on one idea, divide the risk between them. For a 1% limit, that is 0.5% on each: with a 26-point stop and, as an example only, $1 per point per lot, 0.5% of $1,000 is 5 ÷ 26 ≈ 0.19 lots.

Review

What was done well and what to tighten

Done well

  • The protected swing, the zone and the target were all marked before any order.
  • Both entries were at the zone edge, not in the middle of the move.
  • The first stop was beyond the protected swing.
  • The second entry waited for a sweep and a shift.
  • The first trade was held through a return to its entry, and was not closed early when it stalled near the target.
  • Reward to risk was above 1:3 on both.

To tighten

  • The first entry was on the zone’s second approach and had no shift. Record it as an aggressive entry.
  • The second stop sits just under the protected swing. Move it above.
  • No higher timeframe is shown. Check the 1H direction and what the teal zone is before selling into it.
  • Decide the combined risk before the second order, and write it down.
  • The first trade gave back about 85 points of open profit before winning. Decide in advance whether you hold for the full target every time, and keep to it.

Compared with your trade the day before

In the 1H case the sell was also at the zone edge, but with no protected swing marked and a thin buffer. Here the swing is named, the stops are tied to it and the second entry is confirmed. That is progress in the routine, which is the part you control.

Repeatable

The steps for the next one

  1. Has a low just broken with a candle close?Yes. Direction: short.
  2. Where is the protected swing?The high the breaking move came from. Marked.
  3. Where is the supply?The last up candles before the fall. Marked and extended right.
  4. Where is the target?The low just made, or the top of the demand under it. At least 1:2 from the zone edge.
  5. Has price returned to the zone?Wait. Do not sell in between.
  6. Sweep and shift inside the zone?Best case: a high is taken and a low breaks. Sell at the supply that caused the shift.
  7. Orders?Sell limit at the bottom edge. Stop above the protected swing plus buffer and spread. Target a little above the low.
  8. Second entry on the same idea?Only with a new setup, and with the combined risk inside one trade’s limit.
  9. What cancels it?A candle close above the protected swing.

Filters

When this setup should be skipped

Do not take it if

  • A candle has closed above the protected swing.
  • The zone has already been used several times.
  • The low is closer than 1:2 from the entry.
  • The higher timeframe has just broken upward, or price is rising out of a higher-timeframe demand.
  • A second entry would take the combined risk over your limit.

This trade had all of these

  • A series of 15m lower lows.
  • A marked protected swing that was never broken.
  • A supply zone at the start of the breaking move.
  • A target at clear liquidity, more than 1:3 away.
  • For the second entry, a sweep and a shift inside the zone.

Keep this in proportion
