The box that waited four days

Step 1 · Structure and origin

Two legs, two sweeps, one box

Everything the trade needs is on the chart by the 20th at 10:00. Nothing else gets added for four days.

Leg one leaves the first supply (≈ 0.66810–0.66860) on the 19th. The pullback into it on the 20th at ≈ 01:30 wicks to ≈ 0.66820 — over the first X at ≈ 0.66775, inside the box: sweep A, and leg two leaves. Leg two’s proof prints an hour later in two strokes: a body close below ≈ 0.66560, the pullback’s higher low (the MSS bracket), then a body close below ≈ 0.66490, the 19th’s 22:00 low (the BoS line) — displacement, no negotiation.

The candles that fell out of the rally’s top are the box: ≈ 0.66625–0.66725, drawn at 20th 09:30–10:30 and extended right. Notice where its floor lands: within a pip of the white shelf line the pen put on the chart back on the 19th at ≈ 0.66620. Confluence the owner did not have to invent — the market offered it, and the box accepted it. Then price runs to ≈ 0.66100, bounces, and the frame pauses with the box untouched and the whole map — both Xs, both brackets, both boxes — already written down.

Step 2 · The return, slowly

The four-day wait

Between the two frames: ninety-plus hours of chop, one cruel near miss, and a box that never moved a pip.

From the 21st to the 24th the market lives between ≈ 0.66000 and ≈ 0.66500 — under the box, never in it. On the 23rd at ≈ 09:00 a spike wick reaches ≈ 0.66605: about two pips short of the floor. Anyone trading “close enough” buys a wick into nowhere; the checklist’s step 6 says a return means into the zone, and two pips is not into. The same spike leaves one gift behind: its low, ≈ 0.65980, drawn as the red sss line — the sell-side pool resting under four days of chop, the magnet any future fall must reach.

Then the 24th: a rally builds through the afternoon, and at 18:00–19:00 its wick pushes to ≈ 0.66650 — over the range’s highs, into the box’s floor, into the refined red. Four days, one address, one arrival. The pool line had been on the chart since the 23rd: targets get named before returns, so that when the tap finally comes the only new information is the trigger.

Memorise

A liquidity sweep followed by a shift can be enough — but if that liquidity sweep came from a demand or supply zone, the shift that follows adds more probability. The 24th’s wick swept the range’s highs from inside the supply’s own floor; the shift below ≈ 0.66350 that followed carries the full weight of that rule.

Step 3 · Delivery

The tap, the ticket, the delivery

Ten pips of risk against sixty-four of reward, measured at a box that had been sitting there since the 20th.

The refinement keeps the tap candles and the fall’s origin: 0.66626–0.66730 (the axis tags). Entry at the floor, stop above the top — above the tap wick, above the grey box’s own ceiling: a body close there means the supply is occupied and the wick was a break, not a tap. Target 0.65982: the sss pool line, booked a hair above it so the fill lands as the raid begins rather than after it finishes. Risk 10.4 pips, reward 64.4: ≈ 1:6.2 — the ratio a tight refined box inside an old, wide range can give, and the reason step 7 gets measured before the tap exists.

The shift answers the wick within the hour: bodies close below the rally’s last higher low at ≈ 0.66350–0.66400, and the fall never looks back. It crosses 0.66300, 0.66100, reaches the pool line near 25th 01:00 and sweeps it — wick to ≈ 0.65870 — tagging 0.65982 on the way through. At the capture price sits 0.65903: target paid, stop never threatened by so much as a close, and a box drawn four days earlier finally allowed to rest.

The maths

Entry, SL & TP — and why the pool line

Entry 0.66626
Sell limit at the refined box’s floor: where the zone begins, not where the wick ended. The 23rd’s two-pip near miss never touched this price, so it never had a claim on the order.
Stop 0.66730
Above the refined top, above the grey box’s 0.66725, above the tap wick’s ≈ 0.66650: every definition of “the supply failed” lives above this line. Risk 10.4 pips with buffer and spread.
Target 0.65982
The sss pool line at ≈ 0.65980, booked a hair above: the fall’s reason for existing is that pool, so the booking sits where the raid happens. Reward 64.4 pips → ≈ 1:6.2.
The overshoot, noted
The wick to ≈ 0.65870 sweeps past the target — expected, not regretted. A pool sweep overshoots by design; the ticket books at the line and lets the sweep keep its receipt.
Cancel rules
Off on any 5m close above 0.66730 before the fill; off if the pool line is swept by someone else first (target on occupied ground); after the fill, the column’s middle is noise — only the stop speaks.

Do it again

Repeatable steps

  1. Draw the box when the displacement leaves it — origin candles, refined to bodies — and extend it right without shame. Addresses do not expire on schedule.
  2. Put the pool line on the chart the day the pool prints, not the day the tap arrives: the 23rd’s spike named 0.65980 two days early.
  3. Treat a wick that stops short as no tap at all. Two pips is a different trade — usually nobody’s.
  4. Measure the ticket at the box, before any tap: 10.4 against 64.4 here because the refined box was tight and the pool was far. Under 1:2, stand aside.
  5. When the tap finally comes, ask for the shift inside the hour: bodies beyond the rally’s last higher low, or the wick stays a curiosity.
  6. Book at the pool line, a hair above it: the raid is the event that pays you; do not ask it to finish first.
  7. After the fill, one message matters — the stop. Overshoots, stalls and mid-column wicks are the sweep doing its job loudly.

Discipline

When this trade does not exist

  • The near miss is all you get. A wick that stops short of the floor (the 23rd’s ≈ 0.66605) leaves the limit unfilled — unfilled is the gate working, not a missed trade.
  • The tap closes through. Any 5m close above 0.66730 before your fill occupies the supply; the box becomes history and the order stays out of it.
  • Tap without shift. A wick into the box with the rally’s higher lows intact is a raid on the box, not a trigger from it; wait for bodies below ≈ 0.66350–0.66400 or their equivalent.
  • The pool pre-swept. If an earlier leg already raided the sss line and bounced hard, the 0.65982 book sits on occupied ground — take a nearer book at proper ratio or stand aside.
  • Ratio under 1:2. From box floor to chosen book, measured before the tap. A four-day wait does not earn the right to a bad price.
